Verification first.

  • Freeze authority disabled

The website can be published now, but the token contract is not represented as live until its real Solana deployment is completed and verified.

Is there a guaranteed return?

No. AGN is presented as a utility-token project. No return, price or profit is guaranteed.

What should I verify before buying?

Verify the official domain, the published mint address and the on-chain transaction records. Never use an address copied from an unofficial social account.

Can the transfer fee change?

The planned Token-2022 transfer-fee configuration is intentionally changeable by the authorized company governance, within the configured maximum fee.
